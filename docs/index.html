Bubble Shooter: Pop Mania

Our first release: a bright, satisfying bubble popping puzzle with levels, daily challenges and power-ups.

Bubble Shooter: Pop Mania

Drag to aim, release to shoot, match three or more bubbles and clear the board before it reaches the limit line. Easy to learn, hard to put down.

  • Adventure levels with star ratings
  • Classic, Endless and Time Attack
  • Daily Challenge and daily streak
  • Wild, Bird and Thunder power-ups
  • Themes and birds to collect
  • Plays offline, no account needed
