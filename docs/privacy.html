Summary

Bubble Shooter: Pop Mania is a single-player puzzle game. It has no account, login or chat. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or precise location.

We do not sell personal data and do not run our own servers that collect user data. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's policies.
