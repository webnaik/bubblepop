Bubble Shooter: Pop Mania is a smooth, colourful bubble popping puzzle. Drag to aim, release to shoot, match three or more bubbles and clear the board before it reaches the limit line.

GAME MODES
- Adventure: progress level by level and earn up to 3 stars on each
- Classic: relaxed bubble shooting, play at your own pace
- Daily Challenge: a fresh puzzle every day, plus a daily streak reward
- Endless: keep popping and chase your best score
- Time Attack: beat the clock for a high score
- Missions: complete goals to earn coins

POWER-UPS
- Wild bubble: matches any colour
- Bird: a helper that clears bubbles for you
- Thunder: lightning strike that blasts a chain of bubbles
- Swap: switch your current and next bubble

CUSTOMISE
- Unlock themes with coins you earn by playing
- Collect different birds
- Track your stats and progress

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
- Easy one-finger controls with an aiming guide
- Satisfying pops, combos and effects
- Plays offline, no account needed
- Optional rewarded video ads only: watch one to earn a Thunder power-up (limited per day)
